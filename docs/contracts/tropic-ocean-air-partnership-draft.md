## Part B — Agreement

This Strategic Platform Partnership Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of
`[DATE]` (the "**Effective Date**") by and between `[Travail, Inc.]`, a `[Delaware]`
corporation ("**Travail**"), and `[Tropic Ocean Air, LLC]`, a `[Florida]` `[limited
liability company]` ("**Tropic**"). Travail and Tropic are each a "**Party**" and together
the "**Parties**."

### Recitals
- Travail has developed, at significant expense, a proprietary members-only technology
  platform and application for curating, marketing, booking, and coordinating private
  aviation experiences (the "**Platform**").
- Tropic is a U.S. FAR Part 135 on-demand air carrier operating a fleet of aircraft (the
  "**Fleet**") and providing on-demand charter air transportation ("**Charter Services**").
- The Parties wish for Travail to serve as Tropic's exclusive third-party membership-app
  platform partner in the Territory, on the terms below.

### 1. Definitions
1.1 **"Territory"** means the State of Florida.

1.2 **"Member"** means an individual or entity who is a registered, paying member of the
Platform.

1.3 **"Competing Platform"** means any third-party, consumer-facing membership-,
subscription-, or club-based application, platform, or service that markets, aggregates
demand for, sells, or coordinates shared, by-the-seat, or membership-based private charter
access to its members or users, and that is substantially similar to, or competitive with,
the Platform. For clarity, a Competing Platform **does not** include: (a) Tropic's own
direct sales channels, website, scheduled/commuter operations, or first-party app;
(b) traditional charter brokers or charter management companies; (c) online travel agencies
or general-distribution resellers selling individual charters to the public; or
(d) corporate, government, or enterprise charter customers that are not consumer membership
platforms.

1.4 **"Preferred Access"** has the meaning in Section 3.

1.5 **"Travail IP"** means the Platform and all software, source code, designs, user
interfaces, data models, trademarks, brand assets, documentation, know-how, and Member data,
together with all intellectual property rights therein.

1.6 **"Confidential Information"** means non-public information disclosed by one Party to the
other, including business terms, pricing, Member data, technology, and the terms of this
Agreement.

### 2. Appointment & Exclusivity
2.1 **Appointment.** Tropic appoints Travail, and Travail accepts appointment, as Tropic's
exclusive third-party membership-app platform partner in the Territory for the marketing and
sale of Charter Services to Members, subject to this Agreement.

2.2 **Exclusivity.** During the Term and subject to Section 9.4, Tropic shall not, in the
Territory, directly or indirectly: (a) enter into any agreement or arrangement with a
Competing Platform; (b) provide a Competing Platform with charter access, availability, or
pricing on terms equal to or more favorable than those provided to Travail; (c) integrate
the Fleet's availability, booking, or scheduling systems with a Competing Platform; or
(d) license, authorize, or enable use of the Tropic brand by a Competing Platform.

2.3 **Tropic's reserved rights.** Nothing in this Agreement restricts Tropic from operating
its own direct channels, scheduled service, or first-party app, or from selling Charter
Services through the channels described in Section 1.3(b)–(d).

2.4 **Right of First Refusal.** If, during the Term, Tropic receives or intends to pursue a
bona fide opportunity to partner with a Competing Platform in the Territory, Tropic shall
first offer Travail the opportunity on terms no less favorable, and Travail shall have
`[30]` days to accept. This Section 2.4 survives any conversion to non-exclusive status
under Section 9.4.

### 3. Preferred Charter Access
3.1 **Priority.** For confirmed Member requests, Tropic shall use commercially reasonable
efforts to make the Fleet available and shall give Travail booking priority over Competing
Platforms and over channels of comparable type.

3.2 **Response SLA.** Tropic shall respond to Travail charter quote requests within
`[X business hours]` and confirm or decline availability within `[Y hours]` of a booking
request.

3.3 **Most-Favored Pricing (MFN).** The rates, fees, and commercial terms Tropic offers
Travail for Charter Services shall be no less favorable than those Tropic offers to any
comparable third-party partner for substantially similar services in the Territory.

3.4 **No guarantee of aircraft.** The Parties acknowledge that aircraft availability is
subject to operational, weather, maintenance, and regulatory constraints; Preferred Access
is a priority and pricing commitment, not a guarantee of any specific aircraft at any
specific time.

### 4. Operational Control & Regulatory Compliance
4.1 Tropic is and remains the FAR Part 135 air carrier and retains **sole operational
control** of all flights, including aircraft, crew, scheduling, dispatch, maintenance, and
go/no-go decisions.

4.2 Travail is a technology and marketing platform and sales channel only. Travail does not
hold out, and shall not represent itself as holding out, air transportation as a direct or
indirect air carrier, and shall not exercise operational control. The Parties will present
the relationship to Members and the public consistent with this allocation.

4.3 Each Party shall comply with all applicable laws and regulations, including FAA, DOT,
and TSA requirements applicable to its role.

### 5. Intellectual Property
5.1 **Ownership.** As between the Parties, Travail exclusively owns all Travail IP. Tropic
exclusively owns the Tropic brand and its operational systems. No ownership transfers under
this Agreement.

5.2 **Limited license.** Each Party grants the other a non-exclusive, non-transferable,
royalty-free license to use its name and logo solely to perform and market this Agreement,
subject to brand guidelines and revocable on termination.

5.3 **Restrictions.** Tropic shall not, and shall not permit any third party to: (a) copy,
reverse engineer, or attempt to derive the Platform's source or design; (b) use Travail IP
or Member data to build, fund, or operate a Competing Platform; or (c) use Travail
Confidential Information except to perform this Agreement.

5.4 **Feedback.** Either Party may freely use feedback the other provides, without
obligation.

### 6. Data
6.1 Travail owns all Member data. Tropic receives only the passenger/manifest information
necessary to operate a given flight and to meet regulatory requirements, and shall use it
solely for that purpose and in compliance with applicable privacy law.

6.2 Tropic shall not use Member data to market to, solicit, or transact with Members outside
the Platform (see also Section 7.3).

### 7. Confidentiality & Non-Circumvention
7.1 Each Party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with at least reasonable
care and use it only to perform this Agreement.

7.2 Standard exclusions apply (information that is public, independently developed, or
rightfully received from a third party).

7.3 **Non-circumvention.** During the Term and for `[12]` months thereafter, Tropic shall
not directly or indirectly solicit, divert, or transact membership-style charter business
with any Member first introduced to Tropic through the Platform, in a manner intended to
circumvent Travail or this Agreement. (Members who independently book ordinary public
charter through Tropic's own channels are not restricted.)

### 8. Non-Compete
During the Term and for `[12]` months thereafter, Tropic shall not, in the Territory,
directly or indirectly own, build, fund, operate, manage, or hold a material interest in a
Competing Platform.

### 9. Term & Termination
9.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for `[3]` years (the
"**Initial Term**"), then renews automatically for successive `[1]`-year terms unless either
Party gives `[90]` days' written notice of non-renewal.

9.2 **Termination for cause.** Either Party may terminate on `[30]` days' written notice if
the other materially breaches and fails to cure within the notice period.

9.3 **Termination for insolvency.** Either Party may terminate immediately if the other
becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or ceases operations.

9.4 **Exclusivity performance condition.** Travail's exclusivity under Section 2.2 is
conditioned on Travail meeting `[minimum quarterly booked charter volume — $ or segments]`,
beginning after a `[12]`-month ramp period. If Travail fails to meet the minimum for
`[two consecutive quarters]`, Tropic may convert Travail's appointment to **non-exclusive**
on `[30]` days' written notice; all other terms (including Sections 2.4, 5, 6, 7, 8) survive.

9.5 **Survival.** Sections 1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.5, 10, 11, and 12 survive termination.

### 10. Representations & Warranties
Each Party represents that it is duly organized, has authority to enter this Agreement, and
that doing so does not breach any other obligation. Tropic represents it holds and will
maintain all certificates, authorizations, and insurance required to provide Charter
Services.

### 11. Indemnification; Liability; Insurance
11.1 Each Party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its own
breach, negligence, or willful misconduct. Tropic shall indemnify Travail against claims
arising from the operation of Charter Services.

11.2 **Limitation.** Except for indemnification, breach of confidentiality, IP infringement,
or the non-compete/non-circumvention obligations, neither Party is liable for indirect or
consequential damages, and each Party's aggregate liability is capped at `[amount / fees
paid in the prior 12 months]`.

11.3 Tropic shall maintain aviation liability insurance of not less than `[$ ___ ]` per
occurrence and name Travail as an additional insured for the Charter Services.

### 12. Miscellaneous
12.1 **Independent contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors; this Agreement
creates no partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship.

12.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign without the other's consent, except to a
successor in a merger or sale of substantially all assets, provided the successor is not a
Competing Platform (in which case Travail may terminate).

12.3 **Publicity.** Neither Party will issue a press release naming the other without prior
written consent (not to be unreasonably withheld).

12.4 **Governing law / disputes.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of
Florida. The Parties will attempt good-faith resolution, then `[binding arbitration in
[county], FL / litigation in the courts of [county], FL]`.

12.5 **Entire agreement; amendment.** This Agreement is the entire agreement and supersedes
prior discussions; it may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties.
