## What Travail is

Travail is a members-only platform for private aviation. We've built (and
already have live testers on) a polished app that lets a curated membership
discover trips, book seats, propose new routes, and pay — all in one place. In
short: **we bring you organized, vetted, ready-to-fly demand**, and we handle
the member experience, marketing, and technology end to end.

## The opportunity for Tropic

We want to make Tropic the aircraft behind that experience in Florida. Members
fly Tropic; you get incremental, aggregated charter demand you don't have to
chase, with the booking and member-management overhead carried by us. You keep
flying; we keep filling seats.

## What we're proposing (the main terms)

1. **Exclusive membership-app partnership in Florida.** We become Tropic's
   exclusive third-party *membership-app* platform partner in Florida. To be
   clear about what that does **not** touch: you keep all of your own direct
   sales, your website and app, scheduled service, traditional brokers, and
   corporate/charter clients. The only thing we'd ask you to hold for us is
   partnering with another consumer membership app that does what we do.

2. **Preferred charter access.** We get priority booking for member trips,
   quick quote turnaround, and pricing that's at least as good as any
   comparable partner gets. We understand aircraft availability depends on
   weather, maintenance, and your operations — this is about priority and fair
   pricing, not a guarantee of any specific tail.

3. **You stay the operator.** Tropic remains the Part 135 air carrier with full
   operational control of every flight. Travail is the technology and marketing
   channel — we never touch operational decisions.

4. **Clean ownership lines.** Travail owns its platform, technology, brand, and
   member data; Tropic owns its brand and operations. We each license the other
   our name only to run and promote the partnership.

5. **Members stay members.** A simple non-circumvention understanding so the
   members we introduce stay within the partnership rather than being routed
   around it — and a commitment that neither side builds or backs a competing
   membership app during the partnership.

6. **Term and growth.** We propose an initial `[3-year]` term that renews
   automatically. After a fair ramp-up period, we'd tie continued exclusivity
   to reasonable booking volume — we want to earn it, and we're happy to put
   real targets on the table.

## What we're asking of you

- A conversation to align on the above.
- Your team's view on pricing and availability mechanics so we can make the
  member experience seamless.
- A green light for both sides' attorneys to turn this into a definitive
  agreement.

We've already invested significant time and resources into the platform, and
we're betting on Florida and on Tropic specifically. We'd love to make Tropic
the name our members see when they fly.
